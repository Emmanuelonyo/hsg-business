Visual direction · v2
Signal: lime on deep teal

A bank statement, not a crypto wallet.

The SME works in accounts, rates and references. Settlement runs on a stablecoin rail below the line, and nothing from that rail reaches the customer’s screen. Lime marks the action; teal carries the weight.

01Banking words onlyAccount, rate, fee, reference. Never wallet, token or gas on the SME side.
02Money is the heroTabular figures, currency code first, two decimals, right-aligned.
03One status wordTen internal states become five words the SME can act on.
04Shapes, not gradientsArcs, asterisks and sparkles do the decorating. Flat colour only.
